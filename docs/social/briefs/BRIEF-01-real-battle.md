# Brief 01 — Real consenting-user battle (hero format)

Draft: 2026-09-21 · L09 · Parent: `docs/social/CONTENT_EXPERIMENT.md`.

Status: **NOT CAPTURED.** Blocked on: L04 camera/media sign-off, hosted stack +
signed build (L05/R9), two consenting adult testers with signed releases.

Runtime 15–25 s · 1080×1920 · 30 fps · burned-in captions · **real outcome
only**. Reusable for Days 2, 4, 7, 9, 10, 11, 14 and the Day 1 open.

Hypothesis: a real pair accepting and settling a challenge makes a viewer send
the link to their own training partner. Success is a first confirmed battle
(activation), never a view.

| Need | Detail |

## 2. Shoot script

| Time | Visual (vertical) | Audio / caption | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 s | P1 medium shot, phone in hand, looks at camera and names a friend | Caption: "Which friend would accept a 60-second challenge?" | Hook states the product premise, not a result |
| 2–5 s | Real app UI: the challenge link/request arriving on P2's device; P2 opens and accepts | Caption: "One link. No code." | Screen capture of the real build (Session C); no personal data on screen |

| 19–23 s | End card: the challenger's screen with "Challenge a friend" action | Caption: "Send this to your training partner." | CTA gate below |

## 3. Edit, disclosure, CTA

- Edit: no UI compositing, no invented overlays on the app screens, no
  "accuracy" callouts. Only real captured frames of the real build.
- Disclosure: this asset is fully real — no AI label. If any element is
  generated (voice, caption art), apply Brief 03 labeling instead.
- CTA gate: today "Follow for beta updates" only. Replace with the invite CTA
  ("Get the beta and challenge a friend" + link) only when a working
  destination exists (`ACCOUNTS.md` §5) and this specific asset is approved.

## 4. Red lines

No fabricated score, receipt, rating or reaction; no edited or re-timed count;
no outcome the server did not confirm; no accuracy percentage; no
"cheat-proof"; no fitness transformation; no third-party logos or music
without a ledger row.
