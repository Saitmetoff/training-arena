We never ask for your password or for a sign-in code. Support will also never ask you to install something outside Google Play. Treat any message that does as fraudulent.

What to include

  • Your Fault username (never your email password).
  • Your device model and Android version.
  • The app version from Google Play or the Settings screen.
  • What you expected, what happened, and when.
  • A screenshot if it shows the problem — never a screenshot that contains a sign-in code.

Sign-in

Fault signs you in with an email one-time code. If a code does not arrive, request a new one after a few minutes, check the spam folder, and confirm the address has no typo. Codes expire; an expired code shows an honest message and no session is created.

If you opened a challenge link before signing in, the pending challenge is kept on your device and you return to it after sign-in.

Challenge links and battles

  • A challenge link only creates something after the recipient accepts it in the app.
  • If a link does not reopen the app, install from Google Play and reopen the link, or use Join with code with the short code shown on the page.
  • Rounds that cannot be confirmed end as no result and change no rating. That is deliberate: the server never invents a winner.
  • There are no bots and no offline or asynchronous scored challenges.

Reporting and blocking a player

Open the player's card in the app and use Report or Block. Blocking stops invitations and ranked pairing between you both; you can undo it in Profile → Settings → Blocked players. Reports are reviewed manually by the operator — the app never enforces anything automatically from a report.

For abuse that needs a human decision, email us with the username and the approximate time of the match instead of relying on the report alone.

Delete your account

Delete in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account, or use the account deletion page if you no longer have the app installed. Both paths are real deletion; the app is never just deactivated or frozen.

Privacy

What the app collects, and where camera frames go, is described in the privacy policy. Camera frames and pose landmarks never leave your device.
