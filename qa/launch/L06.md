## 5. Ranked and score-event abuse review (read-only)

No physical devices were available, so no ranked match ran. The review below
is a code inspection of the existing protections; it is **not** a device
claim and no cheating-prevention property is advertised.

- Score events are server-authoritative: `arena_match_rep` requires the
  caller's session, a live match and membership; the client cannot supply a
  rating, outcome or user id.
- Replay: `arena_match_events` has `unique (match_id, event_id)` and
  `unique (match_id, user_id, sequence)`; a repeated `event_id` returns the
  current match view without adding a score, and a non-increasing sequence is
  rejected (`AR015`).
- Injection/timing: `observed_at` must sit inside the round window ±2 s and
  camera tracking must be fresh (≤5 s), so precomputed or delayed batches are
  refused (`AR015`/`AR016`).
- Rate: at most one accepted event per 150 ms per player and a hard score cap
  of 240 per round (`AR017`).
- Receipts/ratings: one receipt per match (`arena_receipts_match_unique`),
  rating and history are written only inside `arena_close_match`, and a second
  closure is a documented no-op; profile rows are locked `FOR UPDATE`.
- Queue/matchmaking concurrency: one active seat per user (partial unique
  index), row locks plus `SKIP LOCKED` in matching and the outbox; the 60 s
  seat expiry is server-side.
- Not evidenced, therefore not claimed: camera spoofing resistance, device
  attestation, coordinated multi-account abuse detection or any anti-cheat
  guarantee. Two-phone behavioural evidence is required before any ranked
  claim.
