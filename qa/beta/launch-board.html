Production brief: believable motion and emotion
  1. Generate one adult character and location per concept. Start with a still reference; keep clothing, phone placement and light consistent.
  2. Use a fixed phone-like camera, normal room light, realistic fatigue and complete body mechanics. Avoid long continuous rep sequences; inspect every usable movement for hands, joints, contact and body shape.
  3. Generate the exercise and reaction separately. Reaction: settle on the floor, check phone, small exhale and half-smile after a close loss. Avoid exaggerated facial acting and motion that exposes generation errors.
  4. Composite genuine app footage during editing. Do not bake fake counts, skeletons or invented features into generated footage. Clearly identify staged test results and dramatization.
  5. Export a clean vertical master, then edit platform-specific captions, safe areas and opening cuts. Check music/media rights and generation-tool commercial terms before purchasing production.

No paid generation is scheduled by this file. Select the engine from a short quality comparison using the same reference and prompt; score usable anatomy, motion continuity, emotion and editing cost. Discard clips that fail inspection.
