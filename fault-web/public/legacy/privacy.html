What we collect

Fault is a camera-counted push-up and squat battle app. It collects:

  • Account data — your email address (used for one-time-code sign-in), your username, your rating and match counters.
  • Optional avatar — only if you upload one; avatars are public profile content.
  • Match records — the exercise, both players' scores, the confirmed result, the rating change and the reason a round ended. Rep counts are sent as numbered events with a counter version and timestamps; nothing else.
  • Challenge links and requests — the link token, who was invited and whether the request was accepted, declined, cancelled or expired.
  • Presence during a match — the server records when each player was last seen and whether tracking was active so it can close abandoned rounds honestly.
  • Product analytics — a small set of named events (for example that a challenge link was opened or that a ranked search ended) with limited properties such as a source label. Analytics never carries your email, a session token or any camera data.
  • Blocks and reports — the players you block and any abuse report you send.
  • Push token — only if notifications are enabled in the build you use. The pilot build ships without push, so no push token is registered.

Camera frames and pose landmarks stay on your device

Camera counting runs fully on your phone. Frames are processed in memory by an on-device model and are never written to disk, encoded or uploaded, and the pose landmarks derived from them never leave the device. The server only receives the rep counts, timestamps and counter version described above. No Fault endpoint accepts an image, a frame or a landmark.

The on-device pose SDK is Google ML Kit. Google states that ML Kit does not send your input data or its outputs to Google servers; it does contact Google servers for model and hardware configuration and sends operational metrics (device and app identification, API configuration, event types, error codes, performance metrics, per-installation identifiers and request IP addresses) as described in the ML Kit terms and Google's privacy policy.

What we do not collect

  • No camera frames, photos or pose landmarks.
  • No contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, precise location or background location.
  • No medical records, diagnoses or health measurements (no heart rate, weight or body composition).
  • No advertising identifier, no advertising SDK and no ads.

Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To run live matches, count rounds, confirm results and calculate rating.
  • To let a challenge link or an invitation reach the right person.
  • To keep the service safe: blocks, abuse reports and rate limiting.
  • To understand whether the product works and to fix it, using the limited analytics above.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. The same deletion is available without the app on the account deletion page, where we verify that the request comes from your account email.

Deleting your account removes your profile, avatar, challenge link, pending requests and push tokens, and cancels anything still waiting. Rounds that were still live are closed as no-result.

Some records stay because they belong to the other player's history: the confirmed result and the rating change already applied, including the username and avatar snapshot shown in that result. Analytics events stay without your account id. Backups rotate on the schedule above and are not edited individually.

Your controls

  • Block a player from their public card; blocked players cannot invite you or meet you in ranked queue, and you can unblock in Settings.
  • Report a player or a round from the app; reports are reviewed manually.
  • Sign out at any time; sessions are stored in Android Keystore-backed storage and cloud backup is disabled.
